ABOUT

Evidence over guesswork.

FraudLens checks a suspicious message or URL against a curated library of known Indian scam patterns and returns a risk level with the specific evidence behind it — before you click, share, or pay.

How it works

  1. Rules and pattern matching find concrete signals. Urgency language, requests for OTP/PIN/passwords, suspicious links, and similarity to a library of known scam message templates — these are checked directly and are always explainable: you can see exactly which signal fired.
  2. A spam classifier adds a supporting signal. A pre-trained model contributes a smaller, secondary weight to the score. It doesn't decide the outcome on its own.
  3. The LLM explains the result — it doesn't score it. Once the risk level is already decided by the rules and pattern match above, the LLM's only job is to turn that into a plain-language explanation and a recommended next step.

Good to know

FraudLens gives you a risk assessment to help you decide, not a guarantee. A LOW risk result doesn't mean a message is definitely safe, and a HIGH risk result doesn't mean it's definitely a scam — always verify through the sender's official app, website, or helpline before acting.

No account is required to use the checker. Messages are analyzed to generate a result and are not shared publicly — only the matched pattern category (not your submitted text) may appear in aggregate dashboard statistics.
